## Purpose

[COMPANY NAME] gives you access to internal tools that record production activity, materials and shift handoffs. Before you use them you must read and agree to the points below. This is a DRAFT and must be reviewed and approved by [COMPANY NAME] Quality, HR and Legal before use.

## Confidential information and intellectual property

Formulas, ingredient lists, batch and lot records, supplier and customer information, processes, methods, documents and data you see at work are confidential property of [COMPANY NAME]. Use them only for your assigned duties. Do not copy, photograph, record, send, post or take them out of the facility or off company systems. These duties continue after your employment ends and add to any confidentiality agreement you have signed.

## Accurate and honest records

Record only information that is true, complete and entered at the time the work is done. Never enter or change a record on behalf of another person, never backdate, and never alter or delete a record unless an approved procedure allows it. Report errors to your supervisor so they can be corrected properly. Falsifying a record can lead to discipline up to termination and may be unlawful.

## Your PIN is your signature

Your PIN identifies you. Do not share it, lend it, or write it where others can see it. Everything done with your PIN is treated as done by you. Tell your supervisor at once if you think someone else knows your PIN or has used it.

## Food defense and food fraud

Report to your supervisor or Quality immediately anything that looks wrong: signs of tampering, unexpected, substituted, diluted or mislabeled material, missing material, unauthorized people in the area, or anyone asking you to skip a check or change a record. Do not confront anyone yourself. [COMPANY NAME] will not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Use of company tools

Use these tools only for your assigned work. Do not try to get around access controls, use another person's account, or collect data you are not authorized to have. [COMPANY NAME] may monitor and audit use of these tools and the records in them.

## Consequences

Breaking these rules can lead to discipline up to and including termination, and to legal action. This applies whether or not you meant to break them.

## Statement the person agrees to

I have read and understand this acknowledgment and I agree to follow it. My typed name and drawn signature are my electronic signature and carry the same intent as my handwritten signature.
